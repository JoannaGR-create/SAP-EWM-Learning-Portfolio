SAP EWM Learning Portfolio
Welcome
This repository presents my learning journey into SAP Extended Warehouse Management (EWM), from understanding fundamental warehouse operations to exploring how these processes are represented and managed within SAP EWM.
Coming from a background outside the SAP environment, my approach has been to build a structured understanding of warehouse operations, business processes, EWM concepts and their relationships before moving into more technical aspects of the system.
Portfolio Documents
The portfolio brings together two complementary pieces of work:
01. Warehouse 101 – SAP EWM for Beginners: Before the T-Codes
An introduction to warehouse fundamentals and the business logic behind SAP EWM. It focuses on understanding the processes, their mapping into EWM and the terminology used along the way.

My Approach
The focus throughout this portfolio is on understanding the connection between business requirements, physical warehouse operations and their execution in SAP EWM.
Rather than starting with transaction codes or configuration details, the intention has been to understand why processes work the way they do, what EWM controls and documents represent, and how different warehouse activities connect.
This portfolio reflects an ongoing learning process, built through structured study, practical exercises and the development of increasingly detailed process-based examples.
